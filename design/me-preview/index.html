Me page with Yuni to play with

This is a clickable mock-up. Nothing here changes the real app yet.

  1. Drag anywhere in the sea sceneYuni's eyes and head follow your finger. Hold it high and Yuni reaches up. Shake it fast and Yuni gets dizzy.
  2. Stroke YuniFirst Yuni turns shy and hides behind both wings. Keep stroking and the eyes turn to hearts.
  3. Tap YuniA different move each time. Four fast taps make Yuni dizzy.
  4. Play buttons, now with soundFeed gives +2 pts for your 1 daily fish. Fishing sits next to it. Dance plays a tune in time with Yuni. Turn sounds off under Settings.
  5. Open the shopTap any item to try it on Yuni for free. Fishing rods come first: Bamboo 120, Carbon 200, Golden 400, and the Captain's trident free at Captain level. A better tool finds rarer fish. With the trident you tap Strike instead of Pull. Then hats, scarves, bags, scenes and rides.
  6. Go fishingThe Fishing button opens a game screen. Cast at lunch (11:00 to 14:00) or dinner (17:00 to 20:00), one catch each. Caught fish go in the bucket. One try each: when the bobber goes under, tap Pull 3 times quickly. Too early, or too slow, and the fish is gone until the next meal. Tap a caught fish to feed Yuni: common +2, rare +5, super rare +10. The fish book has 20 kinds, 5 common, 8 rare and 7 super rare.
  7. Wait 12 secondsYuni falls asleep and night comes. Tap to wake Yuni up.
